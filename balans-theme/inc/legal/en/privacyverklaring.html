What data do we process?

We only process data that you provide yourself or that is needed for the website to work:

  • Contact form: name, email address and your message.
  • Appointment form: name, email address, phone number (optional), chosen service, date, time slot and your notes.
  • Quote form: company name, contact person, email address, phone number, desired training, group size and your question.
  • Webshop: name, address, email address, phone number, order details and payment status.
  • Consultations and acupressure massage: what you tell us about your situation. This may include information about your health.
  • Technical data: your language preference and the contents of your shopping cart (via functional cookies, see our cookie policy).

  • To process, pay for and deliver orders (performance of the agreement).
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as the tax retention obligation (legal obligation).
