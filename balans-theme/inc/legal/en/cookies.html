What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your computer, tablet or phone. For example, they keep your shopping cart saved or let the website remember your language choice.

Which cookies do we use?

We only use functional cookies. These are necessary for the website to work properly. We do not use analytics cookies or tracking/advertising cookies, and we do not follow you across other websites.

Third-party cookies

  • Payments: at checkout you are redirected to our payment provider [in te vullen, e.g. Mollie]. That provider may place cookies on its own payment page. Please read its privacy and cookie policy.
  • Podcasts: if you play a podcast via an embedded player from [in te vullen], that service may place cookies. [In te vullen: check which cookies the podcast player uses.]

Consent

Functional cookies do not require consent. If we start using other cookies in the future, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

Deleting or blocking cookies

You can delete or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Please note: without functional cookies, the shopping cart and language choice may not work properly.
